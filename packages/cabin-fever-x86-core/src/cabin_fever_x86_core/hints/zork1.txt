ZORK I: THE GREAT UNDERGROUND EMPIRE
A Friendly, Full-Score Walkthrough


INTRODUCTION

Zork is happy to let chance complicate a good plan. Combat in particular can
produce different results from one game to another, and the wandering thief may
take objects you expect to find elsewhere. SAVE often—especially
before fighting the troll or thief, before boating, and before entering the coal
mine. If a fight goes badly, RESTORE and try again. You can type SCORE whenever
you want to check your progress.

The brass lantern has limited power and cannot be recharged. This route turns it
off whenever the permanent torch can provide light. Do not casually burn turns
with the lantern lit. Darkness is fatal: if the guide says to light it, do so
before moving farther.

The trophy case in the Living Room is your bank. A treasure normally awards
some points when found and the rest when placed in the case. The house will be
our base between expeditions; objects dropped in the Living Room can be picked
up again when needed.


1. THE EGG, THE HOUSE, AND THE TROLL

The first thing we need is the jewel-encrusted egg. From West of House go N
twice, from North of House into Forest Path, then U to climb the tree. GET EGG.
Do not try to open it yourself: only the thief has the dexterity to do so
without ruining its contents. We will deliberately leave it where he can find
it later.

Climb D, then go S to North of House and E to Behind House. OPEN WINDOW and go
W through it into the Kitchen. OPEN SACK and GET GARLIC. The lunch and bottle
of water are not needed for this route, but the garlic will eventually protect
you from a vampire bat.

Go W into the Living Room and GET LAMP. Return E to the Kitchen and go U to the
Attic. LIGHT LAMP, then GET ROPE and GET KNIFE. Go D to the Kitchen, DOUSE LAMP
to save its battery, and go W to the Living Room.

GET SWORD. MOVE RUG, OPEN TRAPDOOR, and go D. The trap door slams behind you,
so LIGHT LAMP immediately. Go N into the Troll Room. The troll blocks every
exit; KILL TROLL WITH SWORD until he is dead. Combat is random, so repeat the
attack if necessary, or restore if you are badly wounded.

Once the troll is gone, DROP EGG in the Troll Room. The thief will eventually
steal it, open it, and carry it back to his hideout. This is intentional.


2. THE TORCH, TEMPLE, AND GOLD COFFIN

From the Troll Room go E, E to the Round Room, SE to Engravings Cave, and E to
the Dome Room. TIE ROPE TO RAILING, then climb D into the Torch Room. DOUSE
LAMP and GET TORCH. The torch is both a treasure and a permanent light source,
so it lets us preserve the lantern for the one puzzle where we truly need two
lights.

Go D to the Temple, then S to the Altar. DROP SWORD here to reduce your load.
GET CANDLES, DOUSE CANDLES, and GET BOOK. Return N and GET BELL in the Temple.
Go E to the Egyptian Room, OPEN COFFIN, and GET SCEPTRE from inside it.

Return W to the Temple and S to the Altar. PRAY. The prayer transports you to a
forest clearing above ground. Go E to Forest Path, S to North of House, E to
Behind House, W to the Kitchen, and W to the Living Room.

READ BOOK now if you want to understand the ritual we will perform in Hades.
The marked prayer and the references to noises and lights hint at the bell,
candles, and book. DROP ALL in the Living Room, then pick up only the TORCH and
LAMP. Leave the remaining gear here for later. OPEN TRAPDOOR and go D, N, E, E,
SE, E, D, D. In full, that is Cellar, Troll Room, East-West Passage, Round Room,
Engravings Cave, Dome Room, Torch Room, Temple. From the Temple go D to the
Egyptian Room and GET COFFIN.

Carry the coffin U to the Temple, S to the Altar, and PRAY again. Return to the
house by E, S, E, W, W: Forest Path, North of House, Behind House, Kitchen,
Living Room. OPEN CASE and PUT COFFIN IN CASE. You should now have 88 points.


3. THE PAINTING, PLATINUM BAR, AND HADES

In the Living Room GET BOOK, GET BELL, and GET CANDLES. Go D to the Cellar, S
across the chasm to the Gallery, and GET PAINTING. Return W to East of Chasm
and N to the Cellar.

Now head N, E, E from the Cellar, passing through the Troll and East-West
Passage to the Round Room. Go E to the Loud Room. The expedient solution to its
acoustic puzzle is simply to type ECHO. Once silence falls, GET BAR—the
platinum bar is a treasure.

Go U to Deep Canyon, E to the Dam, and N to the Dam Lobby. DROP PAINTING here
temporarily, because your load is about to matter, and GET MATCHBOOK. Return S
to the Dam, S to Deep Canyon, D to the Loud Room, W to the Round Room, S to
Narrow Passage, S to the Mirror Room, E to Cave, and D to the Entrance to
Hades.

The ritual must be done in the right order:

  1. RING BELL. The hot bell falls, and the candles tumble and go out.
  2. GET CANDLES.
  3. LIGHT MATCH.
  4. LIGHT CANDLES WITH MATCH.
  5. READ PRAYER while holding the lit candles.

The spirits should now be banished. DROP MATCHBOOK, DROP CANDLES, and DROP BOOK
to make room, then go S into the Land of the Dead and GET SKULL.

Retrace your route N, U, N, N, N, W, W, S, U: Entrance to Hades, Cave, Mirror
Room, Narrow Passage, Round Room, East-West Passage, Troll Room, Cellar, Living
Room. PUT SKULL IN CASE and PUT BAR IN CASE. Your score should be 127.


4. THE MAZE, THE CYCLOPS, AND A SHORTCUT HOME

From the Living Room go D, N, then W from the Troll Room to enter the maze.
Follow this route exactly:

    W, W, W, U

GET KNIFE (the rusty knife beside the skeleton) and GET BAG (the leather bag of
coins), but do not disturb the skeleton itself. Then go:

    SW, E, S, SE

You arrive in the Cyclops Room. Say ODYSSEUS. The cyclops flees in terror and,
more importantly, smashes open a permanent route to the house. Go E through
the new Strange Passage and E again to the Living Room. PUT BAG IN CASE and
DROP RUSTY KNIFE. This western door will save a great deal of travel later.


5. OPENING THE DAM

Go D, N, E, E, N, NE, E: from the Cellar through the Troll and Round Room, then
North-South Passage and Deep Canyon, to the Dam. Go D to Dam Base and GET PILE.
This unpromising pile of plastic is actually a deflated inflatable boat.

Return U to the Dam, then N to the Dam Lobby and N to the Maintenance Room. GET
SCREWDRIVER and GET WRENCH. PRESS RED BUTTON to turn on the room's lights, then
PRESS YELLOW BUTTON. The yellow button enables the dam controls; avoid the blue
button, which only starts a leak.

Go S, S back to the Dam and TURN BOLT WITH WRENCH. This opens the sluice gates
and begins lowering the reservoir. DROP WRENCH; it has done its job.

The water needs some time to drain, so make a brief round trip. Go W to
Reservoir South, W to Stream View, then E back to Reservoir South. Continue SW
to Chasm, S to North-South Passage, S to the Round Room, W, W, S to the Cellar,
and U to the Living Room. DROP PILE and DROP SCREWDRIVER here for safekeeping.


6. ATLANTIS AND THE LOWERED RESERVOIR

Return D, N, E, E, S, S to the Mirror Room. TOUCH MIRROR. Now go E to Cave and
D into Atlantis. GET TRIDENT.

From Atlantis go S to Reservoir North. The reservoir is now drained enough to
cross. GET PUMP, then go S through the Reservoir and S to Reservoir South.
Continue SW, S, S, W, W, S, U to return by Chasm, North-South Passage, Round
Room, East-West Passage, Troll Room, and Cellar to the Living Room.

PUT TRIDENT IN CASE. Then GET SCEPTRE and GET PILE. The sceptre's sharp point
can puncture an inflated boat, so the order in which we load it soon will be
important.


7. DOWN THE FRIGID RIVER AND OVER THE RAINBOW

From the Living Room go D, N, E, E, E to the Loud Room, E to Damp Cave, and E
to White Cliffs Beach. DROP PILE, INFLATE PILE, and DROP PUMP. You now have a
seaworthy magic boat. GET LABEL, READ LABEL if you like, then DROP LABEL.

Do not enter while holding the sharp sceptre. THROW SCEPTRE IN BOAT first, then
ENTER BOAT and LAUNCH. Once afloat, GET SCEPTRE safely from inside the boat.

WAIT once as the current carries you downstream. GET BUOY when it appears, then
WAIT once more and LAND. GET OUT OF BOAT at the Shore.

Go N to Sandy Beach. OPEN BUOY and GET EMERALD, then DROP BUOY. GET SHOVEL and
go NE to Sandy Cave. DIG SAND four times. Stop after the fourth dig—the scarab
is exposed, and further digging risks burying you. DROP SHOVEL and GET SCARAB.

Return SW to Sandy Beach, S to Shore, and S to Aragain Falls. WAVE SCEPTRE. A
rainbow becomes solid enough to cross. Go W to On the Rainbow, W to End of
Rainbow, and GET POT OF GOLD.

From End of Rainbow go SW to Canyon Bottom, U to Rocky Ledge, U to Canyon View,
NW to Clearing, W to Behind House, W to the Kitchen, and W to the Living Room.
Bank the expedition's treasures:

    PUT SCEPTRE IN CASE
    PUT POT IN CASE
    PUT EMERALD IN CASE
    PUT SCARAB IN CASE

Your score should now be 208.


8. THE THIEF'S LAST STAND

The thief should by now have taken the egg, opened it to reveal the clockwork
canary, and stored it in his Treasure Room. He may also have collected the
painting we left in the Dam Lobby. We now need to defeat him both to recover
those objects and to make the silver chalice safe to take. If the painting is
not among the treasures that reappear when he dies, it is still safe in the Dam
Lobby; retrieve it there before trying to complete the case.

SAVE before this fight. In the Living Room GET RUSTY KNIFE and GET NASTY KNIFE,
then go W through Strange Passage and W to the Cyclops Room. Go U into the
Treasure Room. The thief rushes to defend his hideout, and the treasures may
vanish until he dies.

KILL THIEF WITH NASTY KNIFE repeatedly. Several attacks may be needed, and
chance can give you a short fight or a very ugly one. Keep attacking with the
nasty knife while you are able; restore if you die or become too badly wounded.
When the fatal blow lands, his magic fades and all his hoarded treasures
reappear.

GET PAINTING and GET EGG. The opened egg should contain the golden clockwork
canary. DROP RUSTY KNIFE and DROP NASTY KNIFE, then GET CHALICE. (The weapons
are not treasures; dropping them also keeps your load manageable.)

Go D to the Cyclops Room, E to Strange Passage, and E to the Living Room. PUT
PAINTING IN CASE, then GET CANARY; if necessary, use GET CANARY FROM EGG. PUT
CHALICE IN CASE and PUT EGG IN CASE. Keep the canary with you for one short trip
above ground.


9. THE CANARY'S SONG

Go E to the Kitchen, E to Behind House, N to North of House, N to Forest Path,
and E into the Forest. WIND CANARY. A songbird responds to it by dropping a
beautiful brass bauble. GET BAUBLE.

Return W, S, E, W, W through Forest Path, North of House, Behind House, Kitchen,
and Living Room. PUT CANARY IN CASE and PUT BAUBLE IN CASE. You should have 271
points.


10. THE COAL MINE AND THE DIAMOND MACHINE

This is the most inventory-sensitive expedition in the game. You need the
lantern as the light you carry through the mine, the torch as the light you
send down in the basket, garlic for the bat, coal for the machine, and the
screwdriver to operate it. The narrow Timber Room passage will admit you only
while you carry nothing at all.

In the Living Room GET SCREWDRIVER and GET GARLIC. You should already be
carrying both the lamp and the torch from the earlier expeditions; pick either
one up here if you happened to leave it behind. Go D, N, E, E, S, S to the
Mirror Room and TOUCH MIRROR. Go N to Cold Passage, W to Slide Room, N to Mine
Entrance, W to Squeaky Room, N to Bat Room, and E to Shaft Room. The garlic
keeps the vampire bat at a respectful distance.

At Shaft Room PUT TORCH IN BASKET and PUT SCREWDRIVER IN BASKET. LIGHT LAMP.
Do not put the lamp in the basket: you need it to navigate the mine, and the
open flame of the torch would be disastrous in the Gas Room if you carried it.

Go N to Smelly Room, D to Gas Room, E into the Coal Mine, then follow this
exact sequence through its confusing passages:

    NE, SE, SW, D, D, S

You end in Dead End. GET COAL, return N, U, U, then take this route back:

    N, E, S, N, U, S

You are back at Shaft Room. PUT COAL IN BASKET, then LOWER BASKET.

Now repeat the outward mine route to the lower side:

    N, D, E, NE, SE, SW, D, D, W

The final W takes you from Ladder Bottom into Timber Room. DROP ALL. This is
essential: the passage west is too narrow for anyone carrying an object. Go W
to Drafty Room, where the lowered basket supplies both your tools and safe
torchlight. GET ALL FROM BASKET. Receiving the torch here is worth points.

Go S into the Machine Room. OPEN LID, PUT COAL IN MACHINE, CLOSE LID, and TURN
SWITCH WITH SCREWDRIVER. When the noise and colored lights stop, OPEN LID and
GET DIAMOND.

To get everything back through the narrow passage, go N to Drafty Room and put
the carried equipment into the basket:

    PUT DIAMOND IN BASKET
    PUT TORCH IN BASKET
    PUT SCREWDRIVER IN BASKET

Go E through the narrow passage to Timber Room. GET LAMP and GET GARLIC, the
objects dropped there earlier. Go E, U, U to return to the mine, then follow:

    N, E, S, N

You are in the Gas Room. GET BRACELET, then go U to Smelly Room and S to Shaft
Room. RAISE BASKET and GET ALL FROM BASKET. Go W to Bat Room and GET FIGURINE.

Leave by S to Squeaky Room, E to Mine Entrance, S to Slide Room, and D. The
slide drops you directly into the Cellar. DROP GARLIC, go U to the Living Room,
and bank everything:

    PUT DIAMOND IN CASE
    PUT TORCH IN CASE
    PUT BRACELET IN CASE
    PUT FIGURINE IN CASE

Your score should now be 330. The permanent torch has served its final purpose,
so it is safe to place it in the case.


11. THE LAST TREASURE AND THE STONE BARROW

One treasure remains in the thief's former lair. From the Living Room go W, W,
U to the Treasure Room and TAKE TRUNK. Return D, E, E and PUT TRUNK IN CASE.
Your score reaches the full 350 points, and a faint voice tells you to look to
your treasures for the final secret.

LOOK. An ancient parchment map has appeared in the trophy case. GET MAP and
EXAMINE MAP. It shows a newly revealed path southwest from the clearing by the
house.

Go E to the Kitchen, E to Behind House, N to North of House, and W to West of
House. Go SW along the secret path to the Stone Barrow. Finally, go W through
the open stone door.

The door closes behind you, the game congratulates you on completing a great
and perilous adventure, and your final rank is Master Adventurer: 350 points
out of 350.


QUICK TREASURE CHECKLIST

Before the map appears, the trophy case should contain all nineteen treasures:

  gold coffin                 crystal skull
  platinum bar                leather bag of coins
  crystal trident             sceptre
  pot of gold                 large emerald
  jeweled scarab              painting
  silver chalice              jewel-encrusted egg
  golden clockwork canary     brass bauble
  huge diamond                ivory torch
  sapphire bracelet           jade figurine
  trunk of jewels

If your score is short, compare the case with this list. A missing treasure is
often in the Living Room, the thief's Treasure Room, or wherever the wandering
thief last relieved you of it. Once every item is safely in the case, LOOK for
the map and make the final walk to the barrow.
